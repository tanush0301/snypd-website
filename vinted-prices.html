Short answer

These are typical asking prices on Vinted, not sold prices: the median price of every listing SNYPD saw on vinted.be for each search in the 14 days to 28 September 2026, all sizes and conditions together. For example, an Air Jordan 1 Mid was typically listed at €45, with the middle half of listings between €28.25 and €70.

Searches with no price

The Market Report refuses to give a single price when listings are too spread out: when the 75th-percentile price is more than three times the 25th-percentile price. That usually means one search is catching many different colourways, kids’ and adult sizes, or fakes. These three got no price this month:

  • Air Jordan 1 Low (1,400 listings, €4.99 to €2,000): too spread out for one honest price.
  • Air Jordan 4 (3,626 listings, €3 to €17,000): too spread out for one honest price.
  • Yeezy 350 (337 listings, €10 to €5,000): too spread out for one honest price.

A narrower search, such as a specific colourway, usually gets a usable price.

How these numbers are made

  • Source. Listings SNYPD’s systems saw on vinted.be while watching searches, in euros.
  • Window. Listings seen at least once between 14 and 28 September 2026. SNYPD keeps listing data for 14 days after it last sees a listing.
  • Matching. A listing counts when its title contains every word of the search. Garment words also match their usual synonyms (jumper, sweater, pull, trui), so a search is not split by language.
  • Typical price is the median. Middle half is the 25th to 75th percentile, so a quarter of listings are cheaper and a quarter dearer.
  • Refusals. On this page, no price is shown when there are fewer than 8 listings, when prices are too spread out, or when one seller has more than 40% of the listings.

LIMITS

  • Asking prices are what sellers want, not what buyers paid. Vinted does not publish sold prices.
  • All conditions and sizes are mixed. The Market Report in Pro and Elite breaks prices down by condition.
  • Fakes are not removed. The notes column flags searches where many listings sit far above the rest.
