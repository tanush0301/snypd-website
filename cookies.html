What SNYPD stores on your device, why, and how to change your mind.

Your choice

Checking your current choice...

Nothing in the analytics or marketing lists below runs until you say yes. You can change your mind at any time with the button above or the Cookie settings link in the homepage footer. Saying no later deletes the analytics and marketing cookies SNYPD can reach and reloads the page, so the scripts stop.

What this covers

"Cookies" here means cookies and anything similar that a website stores on your device: local storage, session storage and service workers. SNYPD itself sets no cookies at all. Everything it stores is in your browser's local storage, and only the items listed below.

Necessary

Always on. SNYPD cannot sign you in, open your checkout or keep your dashboard settings without these. The law allows them without consent because they are needed for the service you asked for.

Other services your browser contacts

It stores nothing on your device, but your browser fetches a file from it, so it sees your IP address: jsDelivr (the library that handles sign-in). SNYPD's fonts are served from snypd.store itself. Vercel Web Analytics, which hosts the site, counts page views without cookies or anything stored on your device, so it runs without asking. On pages where you are signed in, your browser also talks to Supabase, which runs SNYPD's database.
