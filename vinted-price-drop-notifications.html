Does Vinted tell you when a price drops?

Updated 7 November 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

Yes, for items you have favourited. Here is what Vinted’s help centre says (read 18 October 2026), what it does not cover, and how to hear about new listings under the price you want to pay.

Yes, but only for your favourites. Vinted’s help centre lists a notification when the price of an item you favourited is reduced, and another when an item you favourited is sold. It describes no notification for price changes on items you have not favourited. And a lower price a seller offers you in a conversation is visible only to you, not on the listing.

How Vinted’s price drop notification works

“The price of the item you’ve favourited was reduced”Vinted help, Managing your notifications (read 18 October 2026)

That line is one of the cases in which Vinted’s help says you may get a notification. The same list includes an item you favourited being sold, and members you follow listing new items. So to be told when one item gets cheaper, add it to your favourites.

Notifications land in your Vinted inbox. Which ones also pop up as push notifications outside the app you choose in your profile under Settings > Push notifications; there you can also set a daily limit for notifications that are not high priority. If they don’t arrive at all, see Vinted notifications not working.

What Vinted’s price drop notification does not cover

  • Items you have not favourited. The help centre lists price drops only for favourites, so another listing getting cheaper sends you nothing.
  • New listings. A new item that is already cheap is not a price drop. A saved search only shows a “+N” counter of new items in the search bar (Vinted saved searches).
  • Offers. Per Vinted’s help on making an offer, a seller can offer you a lower price in a conversation, up to 40% below the original. The new price is visible only to you; other buyers still see the original, and they can still buy until you press Buy now. So the price on a listing is not always the price paid (what you can see about sold items).

Alerts for new listings under your price

To be clear first: SNYPD does not track price drops. It does not watch listings that are already online, so a listing that gets cheaper does not trigger a SNYPD alert.

What it does: it watches a Vinted search for new listings, and a Vinted search can carry a maximum price. So for “Nike Dunk Low under €40”:

  1. Search for the item on Vinted in a browser and set the maximum of the price filter to 40, plus size and condition if you like.
  2. Copy the address from the address bar. It contains /catalog? and the price you set (price_to=40).
  3. Paste it into SNYPD and switch the search on.
  4. SNYPD checks the search about once a minute and sends a push to your phone or computer for each new listing that matches, price included.

About once a minute is how often SNYPD looks, not how fast a listing reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in search a few minutes after it is posted. Which one to use

  • One item you are waiting on: favourite it. Vinted tells you when its price is reduced or when it sells.
  • Any new listing of a model under your price: a Vinted search with a maximum price, watched by SNYPD.
  • The two work side by side: favourites for what is already listed, a search for what gets listed next.

Questions

Does Vinted notify you when a price drops?

Yes, for items you have favourited. Vinted’s help centre lists a notification when the price of an item you favourited is reduced, and another when it is sold.

Do I get a notification if an item I have not favourited drops in price?

Vinted’s help centre describes none. Its price drop notification is for favourites, so add the item to your favourites if you want to hear about it.

Does SNYPD track price drops on Vinted?

No. SNYPD alerts you to new listings that match your Vinted search, including a maximum price you set. It does not watch prices of listings that are already online.

Can other buyers see the lower price a seller offered me?

No. According to Vinted’s help, a lower price a seller sends in a conversation is visible only to that buyer; everyone else still sees the original price.
