How it works

  1. Copy your search. On Vinted, set your filters (brand, size, price, condition) and copy the link from the address bar.
  2. Paste it into SNYPD. Every filter in the link is kept. A link SNYPD cannot read in full is refused, not trimmed.
  3. Get a push. SNYPD checks the search about every 30 seconds and sends a push notification when a new listing matches. You open it on Vinted and decide.

What SNYPD never does

  • Never asks for your Vinted password and never signs in to your Vinted account.
  • Never buys, makes offers or messages sellers. Every purchase is yours to make on Vinted.
  • No Telegram, Discord or email alerts: push notifications and the dashboard only.

Where it works

Every Vinted site in Europe and the US: 26 sites. Alerts arrive on your phone or computer. On iPhone they work after you add SNYPD to your Home Screen, because Apple only allows web push there.
