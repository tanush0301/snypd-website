HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Then your phone does the watching.

  1. 1

    Say what you want

    Type a few words and a maximum price, or paste any Vinted search link. Every filter you set on Vinted is kept: brand, size, condition, colour.

  2. 2

    SNYPD checks Vinted for you

    About once a minute. During your free trial and on paid plans it keeps checking around the clock, with your phone in your pocket.

  3. 3

    You get the alert and buy on Vinted

    Tap it, open the listing on Vinted and buy it yourself. SNYPD never buys and never messages sellers for you.

QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Does SNYPD touch my Vinted account?

No. It reads public listings only. It never logs in, buys or messages.

Does it run with my laptop closed?

Paid plans, always. Free: 24/7 for the first 3 days after you save your first search, then only while your dashboard is open.

How fast are alerts?

About every 60 s (measured median), up to 5 min on quiet searches. Same on every plan. One batched push.

Which markets?

Every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 in all. Prices in that site's currency.

How long do matches stay?

About 30 minutes while listed. The alert on your phone keeps what the feed drops.

Alerts on iPhone?

Add SNYPD to your Home Screen from Safari first. Apple requires that for web alerts.
