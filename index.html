QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Does SNYPD touch my Vinted account?

No. It reads public listings only. It never logs in, buys or messages.

Why does an alert have no price rating?

The rating needs 30 finds in the same condition in one week. A busy search gets there in a day or two. A narrow one, like one exact item in one size, often has fewer. The alert still arrives, just without the rating.

Are these the prices items sold for?

No. They are asking prices of listings SNYPD saw. Vinted does not publish what items sell for, so SNYPD does not show sale prices or guess them.

Does it run with my laptop closed?

Paid plans, always. Free: 24/7 for the first 3 days after you save your first search, then only while your dashboard is open.

How fast are alerts?

About every 60 s (measured median), up to 5 min on quiet searches. Same on every plan. One batched push.

Which markets?

Alerts and the price rating: every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 in all. What's moving and Check a price: EU listings, in euros.

How long do matches stay?

About 30 minutes while listed. The alert on your phone keeps what the feed drops.

Alerts on iPhone?

Add SNYPD to your Home Screen from Safari first. Apple requires that for web alerts.
