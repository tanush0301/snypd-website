8 reasons resellers choose SNYPD

01

Pro (€24.99 a month) includes the Market Report: type an item and see its typical Vinted asking price, split by condition. It shows no price at all when the data is too thin or too mixed to trust.

02

SNYPD needs an email address and nothing else: no Vinted password, no login, no browser extension. It never buys and never messages sellers. You buy through Vinted’s normal checkout.

Paste a Vinted search link and SNYPD keeps every filter in it: brand, size, price, condition, colour, material, category. If you can filter it on Vinted, SNYPD keeps that filter.

Souk builds alerts from its own form (brand, category, size, keywords, price). SNYPD works on every Vinted site in Europe and the US: 26 sites, each in its own currency.

No tool is best at everything. Three things other Vinted bots offer that SNYPD does not, from each tool’s own site:

  • They can buy for you.Souk, V-Tools, Resell Reserve, Resell Vault, VINTI, VintageLab and CopMarket (Pro) sell autobuy. Buying on Vinted needs a logged-in account, so those features act through yours. SNYPD never signs in as you, so every purchase stays your decision, made on Vinted.
  • They alert you in Telegram, Discord or their own app.Telvin sends alerts in Telegram; Resell Reserve, Resell Vault, Monitorius and VintageLab in Discord; InstantAlert, Vinotify and DealFindr in their own apps; MarktAlert by Telegram, email or webhooks. SNYPD sends push notifications to your phone or computer only: nothing to install, but no Telegram or Discord option.
  • Their first paid plan costs less.Vinotify starts at €2.99 a month, Telvin at €4.90, InstantAlert and DealFindr at €4.99. SNYPD’s first paid plan, Solo, is €5.99 and watches 1 search 24/7; the Market Report starts with Pro at €24.99. Without a subscription, the Hunt Pass is €3.99 once for 1 search over 30 days.

What about speed?

Telvin advertises alerts in 750 ms on its paid plans, Resell Reserve detection in under 0.15 s, Resell Vault “0.14ms”, V-Tools detection “in a few milliseconds”, Bot Vinted “2,2 secondes”, InstantAlert “within 60 seconds”, DealFindr a check every 5 to 60 minutes depending on the plan, and Crazed “temps réel” (real time). Those are each vendor’s own figures, measured in their own way, so they can’t be compared with each other, or with SNYPD.

SNYPD checks each search about once a minute, slowing to at most 5 minutes on searches that go quiet, and the same on every plan. Measured in our own logs (6-7 November 2026, 68,388 checks across 110 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 60 seconds, and 88% of checks came within 2 minutes. That is how often SNYPD looks, not how fast an item reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in its search a few minutes after it is posted, for every tool. What it adds is reach: paid searches keep running 24/7 with your laptop closed, and the alert finds you on your phone wherever you are.

Questions about Vinted bots

What is a Vinted monitor?

A tool that keeps checking a Vinted search for you and alerts you when a new listing matches it. A monitor does not buy: you open the listing and decide. All the tools on this page monitor searches; Souk, V-Tools, Resell Reserve, Resell Vault, VINTI, VintageLab and CopMarket (Pro) also sell autobuy. More in what is a Vinted monitor?

Are Vinted bots worth it?

For resellers who buy in a narrow niche, often. Vinted’s own saved searches show a “+N” counter the next time you open the app, but they do not notify you about each new match, so a well-priced listing can be gone before you look. If you only buy now and then for yourself, saved searches may be enough. Try a free plan before paying: SNYPD, Telvin, Crazed and MarktAlert all list one.

Can a Vinted bot get my account banned?

Vinted’s terms prohibit bots and scraping programs, so no tool can promise there is no risk. V-Tools’ own FAQ says: “No tool can guarantee zero risk.” What differs is what happens on your account: autobuy tools act through your logged-in Vinted account, while SNYPD never signs in as you, so nothing it does happens on your account.

Is there a free Vinted bot?

Yes, with limits. According to each tool’s own site, checked on 10 October 2026: SNYPD Free runs 1 search with no time limit, 24/7 for the first 3 days and then while your dashboard is open; Vinotify’s free plan checks 1 search once a day; Telvin’s free plan covers 1 keyword alert and 100 notifications; Crazed is free with 3 alerts and 30 items a day; MarktAlert’s free plan has 2 alerts checked every 15 minutes; DealFindr’s has 3 searches checked hourly, with alerts 30 minutes late. InstantAlert, Monitorius and CopMarket offer short trials. Open-source scripts such as Vinted Scanner are free if you run them yourself.

Which Vinted bot is the fastest?

There is no fair way to say. Each vendor measures its own speed in its own way (see what about speed?). SNYPD checks each search about once a minute on every plan, and keeps paid searches running 24/7. Measured in our own logs (6-7 November 2026, 68,388 checks across 110 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 60 seconds, and 88% of checks came within 2 minutes.
