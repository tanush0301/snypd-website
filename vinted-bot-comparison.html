SNYPD alerts you to new Vinted listings, shows the typical Vinted asking price of what you’re looking at, and never touches your Vinted account. Here is how that compares with six other Vinted bots, point by point, with the source for every fact.

We make SNYPD, so we’re not neutral. Every fact about another tool on this page comes from that tool’s own website, checked on 1 October 2026, and is linked in the table. Prices change: check before you pay. Spotted something out of date? Email snypd@gmail.com and it gets fixed.

8 reasons resellers choose SNYPD

01

Price data is built in

Pro (€24.99 a month) includes the Market Report: type an item and see its typical Vinted asking price, split by condition. It shows no price at all when the data is too thin or too mixed to trust.

Of the six other tools here, only Resell Reserve offers price estimates, and only on its £44.99 Ultimate plan. See a sample: Vinted prices, September 2026.

02

Your Vinted account stays yours

SNYPD needs an email address and nothing else: no Vinted password, no login, no browser extension. It never buys and never messages sellers. You buy through Vinted’s normal checkout.

Autobuy tools have to act through a logged-in Vinted account to buy for you. V-Tools’ own setup step reads “Lier vos comptes Vinted à V-tools” (link your Vinted accounts).

03

Alerts land on your phone

Push notifications go straight to your phone or computer. No Telegram, no Discord server, no extra account. Several finds close together arrive as one notification, not a buzz each.

Telvin alerts only inside Telegram; Resell Reserve alerts in Discord. On iPhone, add SNYPD to your Home Screen once: Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps.

04

Every Vinted filter you set

Paste a Vinted search link and SNYPD keeps every filter in it: brand, size, price, condition, colour, material, category. If you can filter it on Vinted, SNYPD keeps that filter.

Souk builds alerts from its own form (brand, category, size, keywords, price). SNYPD works with vinted.be, vinted.co.uk and vinted.com today; more Vinted countries are being added.

05

14 days to change your mind

Not happy with a paid plan? Email within 14 days of your first payment and you get it back in full. Prices include VAT, plans run month to month, cancel any time.

Souk’s site states one week. Telvin, V-Tools, Crazed, Resell Reserve and MarktAlert don’t state a refund window on their homepages.

06

Your finds, tracked to the profit

Claim an item you bought and SNYPD keeps it in History: Holding, then Sold, with your realised profit added up for you. No spreadsheet next to your bot.

Part of the SNYPD dashboard. Matches also carry a NEW badge, a DROP badge when the price fell since SNYPD first saw it, and Vinted’s own suspicious-photo warning.

07

Made by a reseller

SNYPD is built by a Belgian sneaker reseller with 150+ sales and 4.5k followers, and run in the open with resellers in its Discord.

Questions go to a person: snypd@gmail.com or the Discord.

08

A founding price, for life

The first 100 accounts, free accounts included, keep Pro at €24.99 a month for life.

The number of places left is counted live on the homepage.

What about speed?

Telvin advertises alerts in 750 ms, Resell Reserve detection in under 0.15 s, V-Tools’ top-plan autocop under 0.1 s, and Crazed “real time” on its top plan. Those are each vendor’s own figures, measured in their own way, so they can’t be compared with each other, or with SNYPD.

SNYPD checks each search every 30 seconds to 5 minutes, faster on searches that keep finding things, and the same on every plan. What it adds is reach: paid searches keep running 24/7 with your laptop closed, and the alert finds you on your phone wherever you are.
