How these prices are made

  • Sample. Listings on vinted.be that matched the searches SNYPD users watch, in euros. It is not all of Vinted, and the listings are not necessarily from Belgian sellers.
  • Matching. A listing counts for an item when its title contains every word of the item’s search; common synonyms count too (jumper, sweater, pull).
  • Typical price is the median asking price. Middle half runs from the 25th to the 75th percentile: a quarter of listings ask less, a quarter ask more.
  • Window. Listings SNYPD saw at least once in the 14 days before the update (it deletes listing data 14 days after it last sees a listing).
  • One listing counts once. Each Vinted listing id is counted once. A seller who deletes an item and lists it again gets a new id, so it counts again.
  • No price when there are fewer than 8 listings, when the dearest quarter starts at more than 3 times the price where the cheapest quarter ends, or when one seller has more than 40% of the listings.
  • Confidence. High when none of these warnings apply; medium when prices skew to the high side (the trimmed average sits 25% or more above the median) or 5% or more of listings are priced far above the rest. This guide only publishes items with a clear price, medium or high confidence and at least 100 listings.
  • Mixed items are left out by hand even when the numbers pass: a brand name alone, several models in one search, trading cards of many sets (9 searches this month).
  • Conditions. A condition gets its own price only with at least 10 listings and a clear price. A missing condition means too little data, not no listings.
  • Updates. Monthly. Each month’s numbers are kept as a dated snapshot, and the Vinted Market Report is built from it.
  • Asking prices, not sale prices. Vinted does not publish what items sell for.

NOT HANDLED YET

  • Kids’ and adult sizes are counted together.
  • Bundles and lots are counted as one listing at the bundle price.
  • Colourways and variants of one model share one price.
  • Fakes are not removed; they only show up as a warning when many listings sit far above the rest.
