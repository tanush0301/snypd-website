What is a Vinted sniper, and what does it do on your account?

Updated 22 November 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

Resellers say “Vinted sniper” for two different kinds of tool: bots that alert you to new listings, and bots that buy for you. Here is the difference, what Vinted’s terms say, and where SNYPD fits.

A Vinted sniper is a tool that keeps checking Vinted searches and reports new listings that match them. Some only alert you; auto-buy tools also log in to your Vinted account and check out for you. SNYPD is an alert tool: it checks your search about every 30 seconds to a minute and sends you an alert, and you decide and buy yourself. It never logs in to your Vinted account, never buys and never messages sellers.

Two meanings of “Vinted sniper”: alert bot or auto-buy

  • Alert bots, also called monitors, keep checking a Vinted search and tell you when a new listing matches. You open the listing and decide whether to buy.
  • Auto-buy bots go one step further: they buy or check out for you, following rules you set.

Both are called snipers because both aim at the same thing: seeing a good listing early. More on the first kind: what is a Vinted monitor?

What a sniper does on your Vinted account

Buying on Vinted needs a logged-in account, so auto-buy tools log in to your Vinted account and check out for you. Whatever they do, they do as you.

An alert bot has no reason to log in. SNYPD never asks for your Vinted password, never signs in to your Vinted account, never buys and never messages sellers. You get the alert, open the listing and buy it yourself through Vinted’s normal checkout.

What Vinted’s terms say about bots

Vinted’s terms prohibit bots and scraping programs, so no tool can promise there is no risk. That applies to every sniper, SNYPD included. Read Vinted’s own terms in the Vinted app or on the Vinted site and decide for yourself.

What does differ between tools is what happens on your account: an auto-buy tool acts through your logged-in Vinted account, while SNYPD never signs in as you.

How fast is a Vinted sniper?

SNYPD checks your search about every 30 seconds to a minute, on every plan. That is how often it looks, not how fast an alert reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in its search minutes after it is posted.

Each tool measures its own speed in its own way, so compare like with like: Vinted bots compared.
