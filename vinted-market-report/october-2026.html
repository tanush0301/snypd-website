Vinted Market Report: October 2026

Updated 7 November 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

What 317,962 vinted.be listings from 185,286 sellers say about Vinted asking prices, 25 September 2026 to 9 October 2026.

ON THIS PAGEKey findingsPricesConditionPrice cutsCondition mixMethodUse the dataData and metadata

Key findings

  1. New with tags asks a median 70% more than very good condition, across the 32 items where both have a clear price.
  2. 6% of the listings SNYPD saw more than once had their price cut while it watched; the median cut was 14.3% (half of the cuts between 8.3% and 23.1%). 487 listings had their price raised instead.
  3. Widest price spread: Jordan 4, where the dearest quarter asks €150 or more and the cheapest quarter €50 or less.
  4. 52% of listings with a condition are very good, 34% are new (with or without tags).
  5. Highest typical asking price of the 38 items: Seiko 5 at €150. Lowest: Dickies 874 at €20.

Based on 317,962 vinted.be listings from 185,286 sellers, 25 September 2026 to 9 October 2026. This is SNYPD’s first monthly report; from November it adds the change since the month before.

Price cuts

A price change is visible only for listings SNYPD saw more than once: 63,322 of the 317,962. Of those, 3,777 were cut (6%) and 487 were raised. A quarter of the cuts were 8.3% or less, half 14.3% or less, three quarters 23.1% or less.

Method and limits

SNYPD collects new listings from vinted.be for the searches its users run, so this covers what those searches return, not all of Vinted. All prices are asking prices: Vinted does not publish what items sell for. Item prices use SNYPD’s Market Report: median and 25th–75th percentile, with outliers flagged; an item is included only with at least 100 listings and a clear price. Prices on other Vinted sites can differ.
