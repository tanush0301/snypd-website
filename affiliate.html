Questions people actually ask

Do I need to be a SNYPD customer to promote it?

No. Anyone can join. If you are a paying member you earn the same 30%.

What if someone signs up free and upgrades later?

You still get paid, as long as they upgrade within the 30-day attribution window from their click.

How established is this program?

Early, and you should hear that from us rather than find out. SNYPD is a small, live product with real paying users, not a large program with hundreds of partners. That is exactly why the rate is what it is: we would rather pay the people who show up first properly than advertise a number nobody actually earns.

Can I run paid ads on the SNYPD brand name?

No. Bidding on our own brand terms means we pay commission on customers who were already looking for us. Everything else is fair game.
