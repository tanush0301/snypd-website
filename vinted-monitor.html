What is a Vinted monitor?

Updated 9 October 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

A Vinted monitor watches your Vinted searches and tells you when something new matches. Here is how monitors work, how they differ from autobuy bots and seller tools, what to check before you pick one, and where SNYPD fits.

The short answer

A Vinted monitor is a tool that checks your Vinted searches over and over and alerts you when a listing appears that was not there before. Resellers also call it a Vinted alert bot. A monitor only finds and tells: you open the listing on Vinted and decide whether to buy. Tools that also buy for you are autobuy bots, and they need your logged-in Vinted account.

Why resellers use one

Vinted’s own saved searches show a “+N” counter the next time you open the app (Vinted help). They do not send a notification for each new match, so you only find out when you look.

Everyone watching the same search can see a new listing the moment it appears, and the first person to notice it gets the first chance to buy. Well-priced listings are exactly the ones that go first. How often listings come up well under the typical price is measured in Vinted deals.

How a Vinted monitor works

  1. You give it a search: usually a Vinted search link with your filters, or keywords in the tool’s own form.
  2. It loads the newest results of that search: the same public results anyone can open on Vinted.
  3. It compares them with what it saw last time. A listing it has not seen before is new.
  4. It sends you the new listing: a push notification, a Telegram or Discord message, or a line in an app, depending on the tool.
  5. It repeats. How often depends on the tool; SNYPD checks about every 30 seconds, less often (up to 5 minutes) on searches that go quiet.

Monitor, autobuy bot or seller automation?

All three get called “Vinted bots”. What separates them is what they do with your Vinted account.

Where the alerts arrive

Monitors differ most in how they reach you. From each tool’s own site, checked on 3 October 2026:

  • Push notifications to your phone or computer: SNYPD. Nothing else to install.
  • Telegram: Telvin Bot sends alerts only there; MarktAlert offers Telegram among other channels.
  • Discord: Resell Reserve posts alerts in Discord channels.
  • An app feed: V-Tools shows finds in its own app.

Whichever you pick, check that the alert reaches you with your laptop closed. A monitor that only runs while a browser tab is open misses most of the night.

What to check before you pick one

  • Does it ask for your Vinted login?A monitor only reads search results, so it has no reason to. Tools that buy for you do need it.
  • Does it keep every filter?Pasting a Vinted search link keeps all of them: brand, size, price, condition, colour, material. A tool’s own form may offer fewer.
  • Does it keep watching when you are not there?On SNYPD’s free plan, a search runs 24/7 for the first 7 days, then while your dashboard is open; paid plans keep it running 24/7.
  • Does it cover your Vinted country?Each Vinted site has its own listings and currency.
  • Can you test it before paying?Look for a free plan or a refund you can actually use after trying it.

Is a Vinted monitor allowed?

Vinted’s terms prohibit bots and scraping programs, and no monitor can promise how Vinted applies them. What you can control is what happens on your own account. A monitor that never signs in, like SNYPD, does nothing on your Vinted account: you find the listing through the alert and buy it through Vinted’s normal checkout. Tools that buy for you act through your logged-in account.

Questions

What is the difference between a Vinted monitor and a Vinted bot?

“Vinted bot” is the wider word: it covers monitors, autobuy bots and seller tools. A monitor is the kind that only watches searches and alerts you. Resellers often use the two words for the same thing, which is why most monitors also call themselves bots.

Is there a free Vinted monitor?

Yes, with limits. SNYPD Free runs one search with no time limit: 24/7 for the first 7 days, then while your dashboard is open. Other tools list free plans too; their limits are side by side in Vinted bots compared.

How fast is a Vinted monitor?

It depends on how often the tool checks. SNYPD checks each search about every 30 seconds on every plan. Measured in our own logs (8-9 October 2026, 32,469 checks across 35 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 30 seconds, and 93% of checks came within 2 minutes. Some vendors advertise milliseconds; those are their own figures, measured their own way.

Do I need Discord or Telegram?

Only if the monitor you pick sends alerts there. SNYPD sends push notifications to your phone or computer, so there is nothing else to install. On iPhone, add SNYPD to your Home Screen first: Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps.

Which Vinted countries can a monitor watch?

That depends on the tool. SNYPD works on every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 sites in all, each in its own currency: paste a search link from the site you shop on.
