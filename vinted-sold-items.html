Can you see sold items and sold prices on Vinted?

Updated 19 October 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

No. Vinted has no search for sold items and no record of what an item sold for: its help centre describes neither. Below: what Vinted says it shows, quoted from its help centre, why the price on a listing is not always the price paid, and how to check a price with asking prices instead.

Short answer

No. In detail:

  • Vinted’s help centre describes three ways to find items: the feed, the search bar with filters and keywords, and following members. None of them is a search for sold items.
  • The price on a listing is the asking price. A buyer can offer up to 40% less, and a lower price a seller offers is visible only to that buyer.
  • Sellers can delete a sold listing once the transaction is complete, so the sold listings you come across are not a full record.
  • What you can use instead: typical asking prices. SNYPD’s Vinted price guide has them for 38 items, from 94,850 vinted.be listings (25 September to 9 October 2026).

What Vinted shows

Everything below is quoted from Vinted’s own help centre (read 10 October 2026). Where the help centre says nothing, this page says nothing.

“Using the Search bar option if you know what you are looking for – the catalogue includes every Vinted listing. You may also add filters or search by keywords”Vinted help, Finding items (article 35)

The help centre mentions filters and keywords for that search. It describes no filter for sold items and no price history.

“The "Mark as sold" button is used when you are selling outside of Vinted’s payment system.”Vinted help, An item is displayed as "Sold" (article 214)

So an item can be displayed as “Sold”. The help centre ties that label to the “Mark as sold” button for sales outside Vinted’s payment system. It does not say that the price the buyer paid is shown.

“You can delete a sold listing once the transaction is fully completed.”Vinted help, Hiding, editing, or deleting listings (article 60)

So a seller can remove a sold listing once the order is complete. The sold listings you happen to see are the ones sellers left up, not a record of the market.

Why the price on a listing is not always the price paid

“Choose the price you’d like to offer for the item, up to 40% less than the original cost”Vinted help, Making an offer (suggesting a different price) (article 258)
“The new price will only be visible to them (other prospective buyers will still see the original price)”Vinted help, Making an offer (suggesting a different price) (article 258)

An offer can take an item up to 40% below its listed price, and when the seller sends a lower price in a conversation, everyone else still sees the original. So the price you see on a listing can be higher than what the buyer finally paid.

What asking prices tell you

One asking price says little. Hundreds of them for the same item show where the market sits: the typical (median) asking price, and the range where the middle half of listings ask. How to check a price on Vinted yourself

  1. Search the exact model in the Vinted search bar and add filters (brand, size, condition).
  2. Compare listings in the same condition. Look at the middle of the asking prices, not the cheapest listing: the cheapest is often the most worn, or not the same item.
  3. Count on room below the asking price: buyers can offer up to 40% less (Vinted help, article 258).

Questions

Can you see sold items on Vinted?

No, not as a search. Vinted’s help centre describes no search or filter for sold items. An item can be displayed as “Sold” when a seller used the “Mark as sold” button for a sale outside Vinted’s payment system, and sellers can delete sold listings once the transaction is complete.

Does Vinted show sold prices?

No. Vinted’s help centre describes no record of what items sold for. The price on a listing is the asking price: buyers can offer up to 40% less, and a lower price a seller offers is visible only to that buyer.

Is the price on a Vinted listing what it sold for?

Not necessarily. It is the asking price. A buyer can offer up to 40% less, and when a seller offers a lower price, only that buyer sees it.

How do I check a fair price on Vinted?

Compare the asking prices of the same item in the same condition and use the middle of the range. For 38 popular items, SNYPD’s price guide gives the typical asking price and the middle half of listings, from vinted.be.
