SNYPD in one paragraph

SNYPD is a Vinted alert bot, also called a Vinted monitor, for resellers. You paste a Vinted search link with your filters (brand, size, price, condition) and SNYPD checks that search about every 30 seconds. When a new listing matches, it appears in your dashboard and you get a push notification on your phone or computer. SNYPD never asks for your Vinted password, never signs in to your account, never buys and never messages sellers. The free plan runs one search, 24/7 for the first 7 days and then while your dashboard is open; paid plans from €5.99 a month keep watching 24/7.

The three kinds of Vinted bot

“Vinted bot” covers tools that do very different things. What separates them is whether they act on your Vinted account.

ALERT BOTS, OR VINTED MONITORS

They watch your searches and tell you when something new matches; resellers also call them monitors. You buy yourself, so they have no reason to act on your Vinted account. SNYPD, Telvin Bot and Crazed are alert tools.

AUTOBUY AND AUTOCOP BOTS

They buy or check out for you. Buying on Vinted needs a logged-in account, so these tools act through yours; V-Tools, for example, states that you connect your own Vinted accounts. Souk, V-Tools and Resell Reserve sell autobuy features.

DO-IT-YOURSELF SCRIPTS

Open-source projects on GitHub that you host yourself, usually posting to Discord or Telegram. Free, but you run the server and fix it when Vinted changes something.

Vinted automation: the buyer side, not the seller side

“Vinted automation” usually means seller tools: they relist or bump your items, send offers to people who favourited them, or write your descriptions. They work on your own shop, so they act on your Vinted account.

SNYPD automates the buyer side only: it watches searches and tells you what is new. It does not touch your shop, your listings or your messages, and it never signs in. If you want your own listings bumped or relisted, SNYPD is not that tool.

How SNYPD works

  1. In a browser, search on your Vinted site and set your filters: brand, size, price, condition.
  2. Copy the address from the browser bar. A search address contains /catalog?.
  3. Paste it into SNYPD, give it a short name and switch it on.
  4. SNYPD checks it about every 30 seconds. New matches go to your dashboard and, if you turned alerts on, to your phone or computer. Several finds close together arrive as one notification.
  5. Tap the alert. It opens the listing on Vinted, where you buy or make an offer.

How fast is it?

SNYPD checks each active search about every 30 seconds. Measured in our own logs (8-9 October 2026, 32,469 checks across 35 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 30 seconds, and 93% of checks came within 2 minutes. That is how often SNYPD looks, not how fast an item reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in its search a few minutes after it is posted, for every tool. Searches that keep producing new items stay at that pace; searches that go quiet slow down, up to about 5 minutes between checks, and speed back up as soon as they find something. The speed is the same on every plan: paying buys more searches and 24/7 watching, not faster checks.

Some tools advertise alerts in milliseconds. SNYPD does not make that claim. What it does promise is that a paid search keeps running when your laptop is closed and your phone is in your pocket, which is when most finds are missed.

What SNYPD needs from you

An email address for your SNYPD account. No Vinted password, no Vinted login and no browser extension. SNYPD reads public listings with its own systems: the same search results anyone can open on Vinted without an account.

Because SNYPD never signs in as you, nothing it does happens on your Vinted account. When you buy, you buy through Vinted like any other buyer.

Questions

Does SNYPD buy items for me?

No. SNYPD finds new listings and alerts you. You open the listing on Vinted and buy or make an offer yourself, through Vinted’s normal checkout.

Do I need to give SNYPD my Vinted password?

No. SNYPD never asks for your Vinted login and never signs in to your account. You only need an email address for your SNYPD account.

How often does SNYPD check my search?

About every 30 seconds. Measured in our own logs (8-9 October 2026, 32,469 checks across 35 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 30 seconds, and 93% of checks came within 2 minutes. Searches that go quiet slow down to at most 5 minutes. Speed is the same on every plan.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, once SNYPD is added to your Home Screen: open snypd.store in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and turn on alerts in the installed app. Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps. On Android, Windows and Mac, alerts work straight from the browser.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free includes one search with no time limit and no card. For the first 7 days after you save your first search, Free runs 24/7 with alerts, like a paid plan. After that it checks while your SNYPD dashboard is open; paid plans from €5.99 a month keep checking 24/7 with it closed.

Which Vinted countries does it work with?

Every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 in all: vinted.fr, vinted.be, vinted.de, vinted.nl, vinted.it, vinted.es, vinted.pl, vinted.co.uk, vinted.com and the rest. Paste a search link from the site you shop on; prices show in that site’s currency.
