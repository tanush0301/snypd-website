How to find Vinted deals before anyone else

Updated 2 October 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

Low prices on Vinted are common; good deals that are still the item you want, seen before anyone else, are not. This guide uses 14,202 real vinted.be listings from 18 September to 1 October 2026 to show how often deep discounts appear, then the four steps that get you to them first.

Short answer

A cheap Vinted listing is not rare. From 18 September to 1 October 2026, 3,400 of the 11,672 new Nike Dunk Low listings SNYPD saw on vinted.be (29%) were posted at 30% or more below the typical asking price for their condition, and 1,904 (16%) at half price or less. What is rare is seeing the right one first. Finding Vinted deals comes down to four things: know the typical price for the exact condition, search narrow with a price cap, get alerted to new listings instead of refreshing, and check the listing before you pay.

1. Know the typical price for the exact condition

A price only looks low against the right comparison. Condition moves Vinted prices a lot: on Nike Dunk Low, the typical New with tags price is 3.4 times the Good one. Typical asking prices for new Nike Dunk Low listings, 18 September to 1 October 2026:

2. Search narrow, with a price cap

  • Name the exact model and, for clothing, the garment. “Nike” alone returns too much to watch.
  • Set your size and condition filters, so every match is one you would actually buy.
  • Set the maximum price around 70% of the typical price for that condition. For a Very good Nike Dunk Low, that is about €28.
  • Sort by newest first, so new listings come to the top. In the search link this shows as order=newest_first.

3. Be first: get alerted, don’t refresh

Everyone watching the same search can see a new listing the moment it appears, so the first to notice it gets the first chance. Vinted’s saved searches show a “+N” counter the next time you open the app; they do not send a notification for each new match (how Vinted notifications work). 4. Check before you pay

  • Photos against the label. Look for wear the condition grade does not mention: soles, creases, stains, missing tags.
  • The size. Kids’ and adult sizes share model names and differ a lot in price.
  • The price against the typical one. Compare the discount with how often that model gets one: on Salomon XT-6, a half-price listing came up 3 times in 14 days.
  • The seller. Reviews and other listings tell you more than one photo.
  • Pay with Vinted’s Buy now button. Vinted says that payments made outside its system are not covered by Buyer Protection (Vinted help: buying safely).

Method and limits

  • Source. Listings SNYPD’s systems first saw on vinted.be, in euros, from 18 September to 1 October 2026.
  • Matching. A listing counts for a model when its title contains the model’s words (for example “dunk” and “low”). This is a simpler match than the Market Report’s, so counts differ from Vinted prices.
  • Typical price is the median first asking price for that model in that condition. Conditions with fewer than 30 listings are left out.
  • Under means a first asking price at or below 70% (or 50%) of that median.

LIMITS

  • Asking prices, not sold prices. We cannot see which listings sold or for how much.
  • Sizes and colourways are mixed, and fakes are not removed, so some low prices are a different item rather than a bargain.
  • One site (vinted.be) and one two-week window. Other Vinted sites and months will differ.

Questions

What counts as a steal on Vinted?

A listing priced well below what the same item is usually listed for in the same condition, that is still the item it claims to be. A price alone does not make a steal: for the models in this guide, between 20% and 30% of new listings went up 30% or more under the typical price for their condition.

Where can I see the typical Vinted price of an item?

Vinted does not show one on its listings. SNYPD’s free Vinted prices page lists typical asking prices for popular sneakers and streetwear, and the Market Report in Pro and Elite prices any search by condition.

Are very cheap Vinted listings fake?

Some are, and a low price is one of the reasons to look closer, but it is not proof. Kids’ sizes, other colourways, wear the condition label does not show and sellers who want a quick sale also explain low prices. Check the photos, the size and the seller before you buy.
