Most of the problems below can be fixed in a few steps. Each section has a button that opens an email with the details we need already listed.

Alerts are not arriving

  1. Check that the search is Active in your dashboard and that new matches show up there. If they do, the search works and the problem is the notification.
  2. Turn alerts on from the dashboard on the device that should ring. On iPhone this only works from the SNYPD app on your Home Screen (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen).
  3. Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off for SNYPD, and that your browser is allowed to show notifications.
  4. On Free after the first 3 days, searches run only while your dashboard is open. Paid plans keep running with it closed.

A listing I expected did not show up

  1. Open your search link on Vinted and check that the listing matches every filter in it (size, price, condition, country site).
  2. Vinted itself adds a new listing to its search a few minutes after it is posted, so a very fresh listing can appear a little later.
  3. Matches stay in your dashboard for about 30 minutes while the listing is up; the push notification is the record of older ones.

EMAIL US ABOUT THIS

My search link was refused

  1. SNYPD keeps every filter in your link. When a link contains something it cannot read, it refuses the link and says which part, rather than running a different search.
  2. Remove that filter on Vinted, copy the link again from the address bar and paste it.

EMAIL US ABOUT THIS

I paid but my plan did not change

  1. Your plan links to your SNYPD account by email: the email used at Whop checkout must be the same as your SNYPD account email.
  2. Your plan updates automatically after payment, and a check every 15 minutes catches any update that was missed: reload the dashboard. If it still shows Free, email us with your Whop receipt and we link it by hand.

EMAIL US ABOUT THIS

Cancel, refund or change plan

  1. Cancel any time in your Whop account (Orders), or Account → Manage subscription in SNYPD. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.
  2. Full refund within 14 days of your first payment for a plan: email us from your account email.
  3. To move between paid plans, manage your subscription in Whop, or email us and we help you switch.
