    How to read the result

    • Typical asking price is the median: half of the listings ask less, half ask more.
    • Middle half of listings runs from the 25th to the 75th percentile. Below its lower end is the cheapest quarter: use it as a filter, not as proof of a bargain, since a cheap listing can be more worn, a different colourway, a kids’ size or a fake.
    • Listings is how many vinted.be listings the numbers come from. Every item has at least 100.
    • Condition matters. Each item page splits the price by the seller’s condition label.
    • Prices on other Vinted sites can differ.

    Why asking prices?

    Because they are what Vinted shows. Vinted has no search for sold items and no record of what an item sold for: its help centre describes neither. And a buyer can offer up to 40% below the listed price (Vinted help, article 258). More: can you see sold items on Vinted?

    Item not in the checker?

    The checker only has the 38 items with enough data for a clear price (at least 100 listings, medium or high confidence, one model per search). For anything else, compare asking prices on Vinted yourself (how), or let SNYPD watch the search with your own maximum price.
