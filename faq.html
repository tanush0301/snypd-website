How do I start?
Create a free account, paste your Vinted search URL (brand, size, price caps), give it a short name and switch it Active. New matches land in your dashboard, and we can send a push notification when a fresh one hits — open it and make the offer.
See plans
How fast are alerts?
Near real-time for new listings — our engine checks active searches as often as every 30 seconds and speeds up on ones that keep finding matches. SNYPD is tuned for “be first.” Noise control gives you one alert per item — re-lists and obvious duplicates are silenced.
Do I need my Vinted login?
No login required. You keep your account private. Alerts open the listing directly on Vinted so you can act fast.
Is SNYPD affiliated with Vinted?
No. SNYPD is an independent tool for resellers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Vinted.
Which Vinted countries does SNYPD work with?
Paste a search link from the Vinted site you shop on — for example vinted.be, .fr, .de, .nl or .co.uk. Prices are shown in that site's own currency.
How many searches can I run, and can I pause them?
Active searches per plan: Free 1, Kickstart 2, Pro 5, Elite 10. Pause any search at any time to free up a slot.
Will I get duplicate alerts?
No: one alert per listing, for as long as it stays listed. Obvious re-lists and stale items are filtered out by design.
Is there a free plan, and how does billing work?
Yes — the Free plan has no time limit and includes one search that runs while your dashboard is open. Paid plans (Kickstart €14.99, Pro €24.99, Elite €34.99 a month, VAT included) keep monitoring 24/7 with your dashboard closed. Billed monthly through Whop; cancel anytime.

Yes. Every plan can join the free SNYPD Discord for reseller Q&A; paid plans get the paid channels. Optional — alerts work without it.

Do push notifications work on iPhone?
Yes, once SNYPD is on your Home Screen: open snypd.store in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and turn on alerts from the installed app. Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps. On Android, Windows and Mac, alerts work straight from the browser.
What is the Market Report?
Included with Pro and Elite: type an item and see its typical Vinted asking price, broken down by condition, based on the listings SNYPD has tracked. Useful for setting price caps on your searches. It refuses to show a price when the data is too thin or too mixed to trust.
