Getting started

How do I start?

Create a free account, paste a Vinted search link (with your brand, size, price and condition filters) and save it. New matches appear in your dashboard, and with alerts turned on you get a push notification on your phone or computer.

Do I need my Vinted login?

No. SNYPD never asks for your Vinted password and never signs in to your account. It reads public listings only, never buys and never messages sellers. An alert opens the listing on Vinted, where you buy it yourself.

Is SNYPD affiliated with Vinted?

No. SNYPD is an independent tool built by a Belgian sneaker reseller. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Vinted.

Which Vinted countries does it work with?

Every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 in all: vinted.fr, vinted.be, vinted.de, vinted.nl, vinted.it, vinted.es, vinted.pl, vinted.co.uk, vinted.com and the rest. Paste a search link from the site you shop on; prices show in that site’s currency.

Can I use any Vinted filter?

Yes. Every filter in the link you paste is kept. If a link contains something SNYPD cannot read, it is refused with the reason instead of being quietly trimmed, so a search never runs with fewer filters than you set.

Speed and alerts

How often does SNYPD check my search?

About every 30 seconds (the measured median), slowing to at most 5 minutes on searches that go quiet. Same on every plan. That is how often SNYPD looks, not how fast an item reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in its search a few minutes after it is posted, for every tool.

Will I get the same listing twice?

No: one alert per listing, and several finds close together arrive as one notification. If a seller deletes an item and lists it again, Vinted gives it a new listing, so SNYPD can alert you about it again.

Do push notifications work on iPhone?

Yes, once SNYPD is on your Home Screen: open snypd.store in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and turn on alerts from the installed app. Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps. On Android, Windows and Mac, alerts work straight from the browser.

Does it keep running with my laptop closed?

Paid plans, always. Free: 24/7 for the first 7 days after you save your first search, then only while your dashboard is open.

Can SNYPD alert me on Telegram, Discord or email?

No. Alerts come as push notifications and in your dashboard only.

Plans and billing

How many searches can I run?

Active searches per plan: Free 1, Solo 1, Kickstart 2, Pro 5, Elite 10. Pause any search to free its slot.

Is there a free plan or a trial?

Yes. Free has no time limit and needs no card. Your first search runs 24/7 with alerts for 7 days from when you save it; after that, Free checks it while your dashboard is open.

What do the paid plans cost?

Solo €5.99, Kickstart €14.99, Pro €24.99 and Elite €34.99 a month, VAT included. Paid plans keep checking 24/7 with your dashboard closed. Billed monthly through Whop; cancel any time. Compare plans.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Email snypd@gmail.com within 14 days of your first payment for a plan and we refund it in full.

What is the Market Report?

Included with Pro and Elite: type an item and see its typical Vinted asking price, split by condition. Asking prices, not sold prices. It shows no price when the data is too thin or too mixed to trust. Free examples: Vinted price guide.
