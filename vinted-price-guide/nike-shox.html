How these numbers are made

SNYPD collects new vinted.be listings for the searches its users run. This page uses the 293 Nike Shox listings it saw 25 September to 9 October 2026: the typical price is the median asking price, the middle half runs from the 25th to the 75th percentile. These are asking prices, not sale prices: Vinted does not publish what items sell for (can you see sold items on Vinted?). Prices on other Vinted sites can differ.
