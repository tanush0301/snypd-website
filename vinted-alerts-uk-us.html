How do I get alerts for new Vinted listings in the UK or the US?

Updated 22 November 2026 · By SNYPD, built by a Belgian sneaker reseller

A step-by-step guide for buyers and resellers on Vinted UK (vinted.co.uk) and Vinted US (vinted.com): what Vinted itself gives you, how to set up an alert for each new listing, and how to keep the alerts useful.

Vinted UK and Vinted US: two separate sites

Vinted runs a separate site per country. In the UK it is vinted.co.uk, with prices in pounds (GBP). In the US it is vinted.com, with prices in dollars (USD).

SNYPD watches searches on both. It works on every Vinted site in Europe and the US, 26 sites in all: you paste a search link from the site you shop on, and SNYPD watches that search on that site.

What Vinted gives you for new listings

Vinted lets you save a search: press the Save icon next to it in the search bar. Its help centre describes what you then get: a number with a plus next to the saved search, counting the new items listed since you last checked. The counter goes back to zero when you view the search.

That counter only shows when you open the search bar. Vinted’s help on notifications lists messages for members you follow listing new items and for items you favourited (price reduced, sold), but not for new results of a saved search. More on this: Vinted saved searches.

So if you want to hear about each new listing that matches your search, you need a tool that checks the search for you.

Set up an alert for new listings, step by step

  1. Open your search on vinted.co.uk or vinted.com in a browser and set your filters: brand, size, price, condition.
  2. Copy the address from the address bar. It contains /catalog?. That link carries all your filters.
  3. Paste it into SNYPD and switch the search on. All the Vinted filters in the link are kept.
  4. Turn on alerts. On a computer or Android phone, allow notifications when the browser asks. On iPhone, add SNYPD to the Home Screen first (Share, then “Add to Home Screen”), open it from that icon, then turn on alerts: Apple only allows web push for Home Screen apps. Step by step: Vinted alerts on your phone.

From then on, SNYPD checks your search about every 30 seconds to a minute, on every plan. That is how often it looks, not how fast an alert reaches you: Vinted itself shows a new listing in its search minutes after it is posted. New matches appear in your SNYPD dashboard and as a notification; tap it and the listing opens on Vinted, where you decide whether to buy.

SNYPD never asks for your Vinted password, never signs in to your Vinted account, never buys for you and never messages sellers.

Push notifications or Discord

  • Push: on your phone or computer, on every plan including Free. No other app or account needed.
  • Discord: alerts in a private thread in the SNYPD Discord, on paid plans, the Hunt Pass and a running trial. Handy on iPhone if you would rather not add SNYPD to your Home Screen.

You can use both at once. Which to pick: push or Discord for Vinted alerts. SNYPD has no Telegram or email alerts.

Tips for useful alerts in the UK and US

  • Set a maximum price in the search itself, in that site’s currency: pounds on vinted.co.uk, dollars on vinted.com. The alert follows the filters in your link.
  • Use precise words: a model name or reference finds fewer, better matches than a broad word.
  • Add size and condition filters before you copy the link, so you only hear about listings you would buy.
  • One search per item you hunt. If you shop on both sites, paste one link from vinted.co.uk and one from vinted.com: each search watches one site.
