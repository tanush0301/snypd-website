A hunter is one SNYPD user with at least one active search whose words name the brand or model, counted once however many searches they run. A row appears only when 10 or more users hunt it. The typical max is the median of each hunter’s own maximum price on euro Vinted sites, shown only when 10 or more of them set one. Users who left hunter statistics in their settings are not counted.

What the list says

  • Brands first. 7 of the 9 rows are brands; resellers mostly watch a whole brand and filter by price rather than one model.
  • Low ceilings. Where enough hunters set one, the typical max runs from €16 to €30. People hunt the cheap end of a brand. For typical asking prices by condition, see the Vinted price guide.
  • Not only clothes. Apple (iPhone) and PlayStation also made the list.

How to use it

If you buy these brands to resell, you are not alone: 126 SNYPD users watch Nike. The cheapest new listings tend to go to whoever opens them first. Measured in our own logs (6-7 November 2026, 68,388 checks across 110 searches): the median gap between two checks of the same search was 60 seconds, and 88% of checks came within 2 minutes.

Set your own maximum from your margin, not from this list: the profit calculator turns your resale price and costs into the most you should pay. A brand with fewer hunters can mean less competition, or less demand: check its asking prices before buying.

Method and limits

This is SNYPD data, not Vinted’s. It covers searches saved by SNYPD users, mostly on vinted.fr (284 hunters); sites with fewer than 10 hunters are not broken out. Words are matched against a fixed list of brands and models, so a search that names two brands counts for both. It shows what people watch, not what sells: Vinted publishes no sales data, and SNYPD does not see sales.

Questions

What do people look for most on Vinted?

Vinted does not publish what people search for. Among SNYPD users on 7 November 2026, the most hunted were Nike, Ralph Lauren, Nike running clothing.

Is this Vinted’s own data?

No. It counts the searches SNYPD users save to get alerts, not searches on Vinted itself, and not sales.

Why do some rows have no max price?

A typical max is shown only when 10 or more hunters of that row set a maximum price on a euro Vinted site.

How often is this updated?

Once a month. Numbers move as users join, change or stop their searches.
