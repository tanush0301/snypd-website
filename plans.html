    How billing works.

    VAT INCLUDED

    The price you see is the price you pay each month. Checkout runs through Whop, which handles the payment and the VAT.

    MONTHLY, UNTIL YOU CANCEL

    Paid plans renew every month. No annual lock-in, no set-up fee.

    14-DAY REFUND

    Not happy? Email help@snypd.store within 14 days of your first payment for a plan and we refund it in full.

    CANCEL ANY TIME

    In your Whop account, or Account → Manage subscription in SNYPD. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.

    USE YOUR SNYPD EMAIL

    Checkout is filled in with your account email. Keep it, so your plan links to your account straight away.

    YOUR VINTED ACCOUNT

    Never needed. SNYPD reads public listings only. You buy on Vinted yourself.

    Plan questions.

    Something else? help@snypd.store, the Discord or the full FAQ.
    Is Free actually free?

    Yes. No card and no time limit. One search: for the first 3 days after you save it, it runs 24/7 with alerts like a paid plan; after that it is checked while your dashboard is open.

    Do paid plans find listings faster?

    No. Every plan checks about every 60 seconds (measured median), up to 5 minutes on quiet searches. Paid plans add searches and keep checking with your dashboard closed.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes. Free runs your first search 24/7 with alerts for 3 days (72 h), counted from when you save it, with no card. If it finds nothing in that time, we add 2 more days once. After that, Free keeps that one search for as long as you like, checked while your dashboard is open.

    Does the price include VAT?

    Yes. €5.99, €14.99, €24.99 and €34.99 a month are the full prices, VAT included. Buying for a company? Tick "buying on behalf of a company" at checkout.

    How do I pay?

    By card, or Apple Pay or Google Pay on devices that support them, through Whop’s secure checkout on this page. SNYPD never sees your card details.

    Does my plan renew automatically?

    Yes, every month, until you cancel. Cancel any time in your Whop account. The Hunt Pass is the exception: it is paid once and ends after 30 days.

    Can I pay once, without a subscription?

    Yes: the Hunt Pass. One payment of €3.99, VAT included, and 1 Vinted search is watched 24/7 with push alerts for 30 days, like Solo. Then it simply ends: no renewal, no subscription, nothing to cancel. It suits hunting one specific item, like a console or a jacket in your size. Full refund within 14 days of the payment. For ongoing hunting, Solo is €5.99 a month.

    What happens when I cancel?

    Your plan keeps running to the end of the month you paid for, then your account goes back to Free.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes. Email help@snypd.store within 14 days of your first payment for a plan and we refund that payment in full.

    Can I change plan later?

    Upgrade here any time. To move between paid plans, manage your subscription in Whop, or email help@snypd.store and we will help you switch.

    What is a founding member?

    The first 100 SNYPD accounts, free accounts included. If the Pro price ever goes up, founding members can keep Pro at €24.99 a month for as long as their account exists.

    Do you need my Vinted login?

    Never. SNYPD reads public listings only. It never logs in, buys or messages anyone.
